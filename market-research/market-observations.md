# Market Observations

This file stores running observations about the 3D printing marketplace.

Use it for broad notes about what seems to sell, what gets attention, what customers complain about, and what product categories may be worth exploring.

## Observation log

### 2026-04-28: Knight and fantasy models appear to perform well

**Observation:** Knight and fantasy models are among the user's best sellers.  
**Source:** User sales experience.  
**Confidence:** High  
**Why it matters:** This category already has demand for the user's style and audience.  
**Possible action:** Continue the knight series, but keep designs printable and visually strong.  
**Related product file:** `products/knight-series.md`

### 2026-04-28: Complex models do not always justify the time investment

**Observation:** A complex articulated modular dragon stand took about two weeks but felt too complicated and did not work as clearly as expected.  
**Source:** User experience with previous model.  
**Confidence:** High  
**Why it matters:** Complexity can reduce clarity, increase testing time, and create weak product-market fit.  
**Possible action:** Score complex ideas carefully before committing serious time.  
**Related product file:** none yet

### 2026-04-28: Practical miniature storage may be a promising category

**Observation:** A miniature transport/storage organizer could be a better practical direction than refining the complicated dragon stand concept.  
**Source:** User product reflection.  
**Confidence:** Medium  
**Why it matters:** Practical utility products can solve clear problems and may create repeatable product families.  
**Possible action:** Prototype a simple version 1 and validate interest.  
**Related product file:** `product-catalog/miniature-transport-organizer.md`

### 2026-04-28: Screw mechanisms can create customer complaints

**Observation:** A previous model sold somewhat better, but customers complained about a screw mechanism not working even though it worked on the user's printer.  
**Source:** Customer feedback reported by user.  
**Confidence:** High  
**Why it matters:** Public models need forgiving tolerances across different printers.  
**Possible action:** Avoid screws in early public releases or provide tolerance tests and clear instructions.  
**Related product file:** `feedback/customer-feedback.md`
