## Analyses

### Miniature transport organizer

**Date:** 2026-04-28  
**Status:** Analyze / Planning  
**Related product file:** `product-catalog/miniature-transport-organizer.md`  

#### Summary

A practical storage and transport product for tabletop miniatures.

#### Market signal

- Solves a clear hobby problem.
- Useful for tabletop RPG players, wargamers, and miniature painters.
- Could become a product family with different base sizes and formats.

#### Strengths

- Practical and easy to explain.
- Can be simpler than the previous complicated dragon stand direction.
- Clear thumbnail potential.
- Strong possibility for variations.

#### Weaknesses

- Needs careful tolerance planning if a lid or locking mechanism is added.
- May need different versions for different base sizes.
- Could become too complex if modularity is added too early.

#### Printability concerns

- Avoid screws for version 1.
- Keep tolerances forgiving.
- Use simple geometry first.
- Consider a small test piece before a full release.

#### Differentiation

The value should come from being clean, practical, reliable, and easy to print rather than overly complex.

#### Time investment risk

Medium

Reason:

- A simple tray is low risk.
- A compact case is medium risk.
- A full modular system is high risk if attempted too early.

#### Recommendation

- [x] Prototype small version
- [ ] Prioritize full system
- [ ] Pause

#### Next action

- Decide tray vs compact case for version 1.
