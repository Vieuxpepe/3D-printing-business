# Knight Series

## Concept

A collection of fantasy knight miniatures and related models.

This direction matters because knight models have already performed well and can become a recognizable product line.

## Product identity

The knight series should feel:

- heroic
- dark fantasy
- printable
- strong in silhouette
- suitable for tabletop games
- collectible as a set

## Design rules

- Always include a stable round or scenic base.
- Use the base to support swords, capes, banners, or weapons when possible.
- Avoid floating thin details.
- Prefer strong poses over overly complex poses.
- Make sure the model reads clearly from the thumbnail.
- Think about no-support or low-support printing from the beginning.

## Possible models

### Temple Knight Commander

A strong commander-style knight with a cape and sword.

Printability notes:

- sword should touch or connect to the base if possible
- cape should merge with the base or legs for support
- round base recommended

### Shield Guardian Knight

A defensive knight with large shield and grounded pose.

Printability notes:

- shield can act as support
- pose should be compact
- good for low-support design

### Banner Knight

A knight holding a banner or standard.

Printability notes:

- banner pole should connect to base
- banner should be thick enough to print
- avoid thin floating cloth

### Fallen Knight Statue

A decorative terrain/statue model.

Printability notes:

- easier than dynamic character poses
- could work as scatter terrain
- good for tabletop and diorama use

## Collection strategy

A strong collection could include:

1. Commander
2. Shield knight
3. Two-handed sword knight
4. Banner knight
5. Fallen knight statue
6. Knight bust or display piece
