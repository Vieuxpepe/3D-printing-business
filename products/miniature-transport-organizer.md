# Miniature Transport Organizer

## Concept

A sleek, practical organizer for transporting tabletop miniatures safely.

This idea is promising because it solves a real problem and can be simpler, cleaner, and more reliable than overly complex gadget-style models.

## Target users

- tabletop RPG players
- wargaming players
- miniature painters
- people carrying small groups of minis to game nights
- hobbyists who want tidy storage

## Problems to solve

- miniatures tipping over during transport
- fragile parts breaking
- messy storage boxes
- foam inserts being annoying or expensive
- different base sizes needing different solutions
- transport systems being too bulky

## Version 1 design goals

- simple and reliable
- sleek look
- easy to print
- low support requirement
- no fragile screw mechanism
- compatible with common miniature bases
- easy to understand from thumbnail images

## Possible formats

### Tray system

Pros:

- simple
- easy to print
- easy to scale
- good for first version

Cons:

- may need a lid or case to become transport-friendly

### Box with sliding tray

Pros:

- stronger transport concept
- feels more like a complete product

Cons:

- more tolerances to test
- sliding mechanism could cause complaints if too tight

### Magnetic base system

Pros:

- strong hobby appeal
- more premium

Cons:

- requires users to have magnets or metal washers
- extra instructions needed

### Modular stackable system

Pros:

- expandable
- good for collections

Cons:

- more complexity
- more testing

## Design constraints

- Avoid tiny fragile clips.
- Avoid screws unless they are extremely reliable.
- Keep tolerances forgiving for different printers.
- Make the first version small enough to prototype quickly.
- Design around common base sizes first.

## First prototype idea

A compact tray that holds a small team of miniatures, with rounded slots and a clean protective border.

Possible first target:

- 25 mm bases
- 32 mm bases
- 40 mm bases
