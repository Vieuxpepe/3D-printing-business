## MakerWorld description draft

### Short description

A detailed female warrior miniature designed for tabletop RPGs, fantasy campaigns, painting, and display. Great as a player character, NPC, fighter, knight, mercenary, or heroic fantasy adventurer.

### Full description

Bring a strong fantasy warrior to your tabletop with this detailed female warrior miniature. The model is designed for tabletop RPG players, miniature painters, fantasy collectors, and game masters who want a versatile heroic character for their campaign.

This miniature can be used as a fighter, knight, mercenary, adventurer, guard captain, champion, or fantasy NPC. The pose is intended to read clearly on the table while still offering enough detail for painting and display.

The model is suitable for Dungeons & Dragons-style campaigns, Pathfinder-style games, fantasy skirmish games, dioramas, and general tabletop collections.

### Suggested uses

- player character miniature
- female fighter or warrior
- knight or mercenary NPC
- fantasy guard captain
- tabletop RPG encounter piece
- miniature painting practice
- fantasy display miniature

### Print notes

Recommended to print with fine detail settings for best miniature quality. Check the uploaded print profile for suggested orientation, supports, and layer height.

Support removal should be done carefully around smaller details such as weapons, armor edges, hands, and base contact points.
