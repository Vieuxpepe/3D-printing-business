## Short description

A practical 3D printable organizer designed to help tabletop players store and transport miniatures safely.

## Full concept

The miniature transport organizer is meant to be a clean, practical, and reliable storage solution for tabletop miniatures.

The goal is to create something simpler and more useful than an overcomplicated gadget. Version 1 should focus on solving the core problem: keeping miniatures organized and safer during transport.

The first version should likely be a simple tray or compact case rather than a full modular system.

## Target users

- tabletop RPG players
- wargaming players
- miniature painters
- hobbyists who carry miniatures to game nights
- people who want cleaner miniature storage

## Problem solved

- miniatures tipping over during transport
- fragile parts getting damaged
- messy storage boxes
- foam inserts being expensive or annoying
- different base sizes needing better organization

## Main selling points

- practical and useful
- cleaner than loose storage boxes
- easier to print than complex mechanisms
- expandable into a product family
- can target common miniature base sizes

## Design notes

### Visual direction

- clean
- sleek
- practical
- minimal for version 1
- optional fantasy-themed version later

### Functional requirements

- hold miniatures securely enough for normal transport
- support common base sizes
- avoid unreliable screw mechanisms
- be easy to understand from thumbnail images

### Printability requirements

- low support requirement
- forgiving tolerances
- strong walls around miniature slots
- no tiny fragile clips in version 1
- easy to print on common FDM printers

### Risks

- tolerances may be printer-dependent
- too many base sizes could make version 1 too broad
- a compact case may require more testing than a tray
- magnets could complicate instructions if used too early

## Print settings

**Printer type:** FDM  
**Material:** PLA or PETG  
**Layer height:** 0.2 mm starting point  
**Infill:** 10% to 20% depending on wall structure  
**Supports:** no or minimal  
**Assembly required:** ideally no  
**Tolerance-sensitive:** maybe, depending on lid or fit system
