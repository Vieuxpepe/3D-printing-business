# Dark Fantasy Shieldmaiden Knight Miniature

## Concept

A dark fantasy shieldmaiden knight miniature designed for tabletop RPGs, display prints, painting, and fantasy collections. Features a strong round base, detailed armor, shield, sword, and gritty heroic silhouette.

## Features

- Armored shieldmaiden warrior design
- Round scenic base included
- Sword and shield equipment
- Spiked shoulder armor
- Layered fantasy armor and cloth details
- Works at miniature scale and larger display scale
- Good silhouette for thumbnails, tabletop readability, and painted display

## Print Profile Notes

**Recommended material:** PLA / PETG / resin  
**Supports:** yes  
**Layer height:** 0.08 mm recommended for FDM, 0.03-0.05 mm for resin  
**Infill:** 15-20% for FDM  
**Estimated print time:** varies by scale and printer

## Assembly

- None required; prints as a single piece with integrated base.

## Tips

- Use tree supports for shield, sword, hair, arms, and shoulder spikes
- Let FDM prints cool before removing supports
- Small prints need careful support removal
- Print larger for easier painting
