## Current rule

Do not start a huge complex model immediately after an exhausting semester. Pick a smaller, practical model that can rebuild momentum.

## Focus filter

Before starting a model, ask:

1. Does it solve a clear problem?
2. Can the thumbnail explain the product quickly?
3. Can it print reliably?
4. Can it be finished in a reasonable time?
5. Does it have a reason to exist beyond being easy to model?
