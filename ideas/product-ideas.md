# Product Ideas

This file is for capturing raw and organized 3D printing product ideas.

## Priority system

- **High**: worth exploring soon
- **Medium**: promising, but needs validation
- **Low**: interesting, but not urgent
- **Paused**: not worth doing right now

## High priority ideas

### Miniature transport and storage organizer

**Status:** promising concept  
**Why it matters:** practical, useful, easier to explain, and likely more reliable than complicated decorative mechanisms.

Possible directions:

- modular miniature trays
- magnetic base support
- stackable case system
- foam-free transport insert
- faction/team organizer
- travel-friendly miniature box

Key design goals:

- sleek and simple
- reliable printability
- no annoying screws if avoidable
- strong enough for transport
- easy to scale for different miniature sizes

## Medium priority ideas

## Low priority ideas

### Practical gaming accessories

Possible directions:

- controller holders
- cartridge organizers
- tabletop accessories
- dice trays
- compact storage systems

## Notes from past experience

- Overly complicated models can take too long and may not sell proportionally better.
- If a mechanism creates customer complaints, simplify it.
- A simpler, cleaner design can outperform a complex one if it solves the problem better.
- Print reliability should be treated as part of the product, not an afterthought.
