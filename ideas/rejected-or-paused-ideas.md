# Rejected or Paused Ideas

This file is for ideas that are not the best use of time right now.

The goal is not to delete ideas forever. The goal is to avoid wasting energy on designs that are too complicated, too fragile, too easy to copy, or not practical enough.

## Paused ideas

### Complex articulated dragon stand concept

**Status:** paused  
**Reason:** required a lot of work, looked complicated, and did not function as clearly as expected.

Lessons:

- complexity does not automatically create value
- the model needs to communicate its function instantly
- if the user does not understand what it does from the thumbnail, sales may suffer
- complicated designs create more testing and support problems

### Very simple clips

**Status:** rejected for now  
**Reason:** too easy to make and likely too generic.

Lessons:

- simple is good, but not if the idea has no unique value
- practical products still need a clear design advantage

## Ideas to revisit only with a strong angle

- generic controller stands
- basic hooks or clips
- overcomplicated mechanical gadgets
- designs requiring fragile screws or tight tolerances

## Decision rule

Pause an idea when at least two of these are true:

- it is hard to explain visually
- it requires too many test prints
- it depends on tight tolerances
- it solves a weak problem
- it is easy for anyone to copy quickly
- it creates likely customer complaints
- it would take more than one week without clear upside
