# Future Opportunities

This file is for ideas that could become interesting later, but are not the current focus.

## Marketplace opportunities

### MakerWorld

Potential advantages:

- strong discovery for printable models
- good for free or profile-building releases
- useful for testing demand
- good place to build a recognizable catalog

Things to watch:

- which models get downloads quickly
- comments and complaints
- remix potential
- print profile quality

### Cults3D

Potential advantages:

- paid model marketplace
- useful for polished premium products
- good for niche models with clear value

Things to watch:

- keyword density in descriptions
- thumbnails and renders
- clear print instructions
- avoiding customer confusion
